Limitations

  • Tiny sample. The data has 15 customers. One customer changes a segment's churn rate by several points.
  • The clean split is not proof. Every High-tier customer churned and no Medium or Low customer did. The weights were chosen after looking at this same data, so this shows the rules describe the sample, not that they predict new customers.
  • Impact ranges are assumptions. The 10–30% retention figures are placeholders for planning. They were not measured in this data and should be replaced after a real test.
  • Missing signals. The data has no usage, billing disputes or competitor offers, and it is a single snapshot, so it cannot show how behavior changed before a customer left.
  • Next step. Re-check the weights on a larger, held-out set of customers before using the score to decide who gets contacted.
